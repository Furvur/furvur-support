Privacy Policy

Whittle converts photos, video, and audio entirely on your own Mac, using the media frameworks built into macOS. Your files never leave your computer.

  • No data collection. We do not collect personal information, usage data, analytics, or any identifiers.
  • No accounts. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No file uploads. Every conversion happens on-device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • No third-party tracking. No SDKs, no telemetry, no ads.
  • No network requests. Whittle makes no network connections at all and works fully offline.

Your photos, videos, and audio stay on your Mac. We never see them.

File Access

Whittle can read a file only when you drag it onto the window or select it yourself. It has no ability to browse your Mac on its own. Converted files are written only to the folder you choose — your Downloads folder by default.

Local Storage

Whittle stores a small amount of data locally on your Mac:

  • Your chosen output formats and quality settings
  • The destination folder you selected

This data never leaves your Mac and is never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the Mac App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions.

Photo Metadata

When converting images, Whittle copies the original image data across rather than re-encoding it, so information already in the file — date taken, camera, and location — is preserved in the converted copy. We do not read, collect, or transmit this data.

Children's Privacy

Whittle does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the date above. Given that we collect no data, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Common Questions

Where do my converted files go?
Your Downloads folder by default. Use Change… next to “Save to” to pick a different folder, and Whittle will remember it.

Why can't Whittle save next to the original file?
Mac App Store apps run in a sandbox. Dragging a file in gives Whittle permission to read that one file, not to write into the folder it came from. Choosing a destination folder once grants that permission properly.

What's the difference between JPEG and JPG?
Nothing but the file extension. The two produce identical files.

Why is my PNG or WAV larger than the original?
PNG, TIFF, and WAV are lossless formats — they store everything without compression loss, so they are larger than compressed sources like HEIC or MP3. That is the format working as intended.

Can Whittle export MP3?
No. macOS can read MP3 but cannot write it, and adding an encoder would mean bundling third-party code. M4A plays everywhere MP3 does and sounds better at the same file size.

Can Whittle open MKV or AVI files?
Not currently. Whittle uses the formats macOS supports natively, which is what keeps it small and fast with no third-party code inside.

Why is my GIF so large?
GIF stores each frame almost whole, with no compression between frames, so size grows with length. For anything long, a small MP4 is usually the better choice.
