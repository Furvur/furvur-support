Privacy Policy

Spoke adds captions to your videos entirely on your iPhone, using a speech model that ships inside the app. Your videos never leave your device.

  • No data collection. We do not collect personal information, usage data, analytics, or any identifiers.
  • No accounts. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No uploads. Every video is transcribed and rendered on-device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • No third-party tracking. No SDKs, no telemetry, no ads, and no use of the Advertising Identifier.
  • No network requests other than the App Store's own handling of the in-app purchase. Captioning works in aeroplane mode.

Your videos stay on your device. We never see them, and neither does anyone else.

Videos You Choose

Spoke reads a video only when you pick it yourself from your photo library. It cannot browse your library on its own and does not look at anything beyond the single video you choose. A working copy is kept in the app's temporary storage while the video is being processed and is deleted afterwards.

When you export, Spoke asks permission to add the finished video to your photo library. That is the only thing it writes there.

Local Storage

Spoke keeps a small amount of information on your device and nowhere else: your caption style, size, animation and language preferences, and how many of your free exports you have used. Whether you have bought the unlimited-exports unlock is held by the App Store, not by us. All of it is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Apple tells Spoke only whether a purchase exists. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions.

Children's Privacy

Spoke does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the date above. Given that we collect no data, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Common Questions

What does Spoke actually do?

You pick a video. Spoke listens to it, writes out every word, and times each one to the audio. You choose a style, check it against the video, and export. The captions are burned into the exported file, so it is ready to post anywhere.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription runs on your iPhone using a model bundled inside the app. There is no server involved at any point. Turn on aeroplane mode and it still works.

Do I have to download anything the first time?

No. The speech model ships inside the app, which is why the download is larger than most. There is no setup and no waiting.

How much is free?

Your first three exports are free. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited exports. It is not a subscription. Only an export that actually reaches your photo library counts — a cancelled or failed one does not.

Do exported videos have a watermark?

No. What you export is what you see, with nothing added.

Can I fix a word Spoke got wrong?

Not in this version. The preview is there to show you what you are getting rather than to edit it. If a clip is short or noisy and the words come out wrong, choosing the language in Settings instead of leaving it on auto-detect usually helps.

What languages does it handle?

Spoke detects the language automatically and handles the ones the bundled Whisper model supports. You can also set the language yourself in Settings, which tends to be more accurate on short or noisy clips.

Does changing the style start everything over?

No. The transcript is done once. Switching style, size, highlighting or animation only changes how it is drawn, so you can change your mind as often as you like.

Where does the exported video go?

Straight to your photo library, once you give permission.
